# BBuidl Lab mascot system

The first mascot family is built from the reference character's strongest cues: oversized floppy ears, a soft rounded body, a triangular black nose, pink cheeks, and a curved tail.

## The five characters

- **Bubi** — the heart of the lab. Warm, dependable, and welcoming; ideal for onboarding, community, and homepage moments.
- **Loopi** — the systems thinker. Lavender, circular, and structured; ideal for engineering, infrastructure, and workflows.
- **Sprig** — the growth mindset. Mint green with a branching chest mark; ideal for learning, iteration, and experiments.
- **Piko** — the fast prototyper. Coral with a yellow tool belt; ideal for demos, launches, and shipping culture.
- **Nori** — the brave explorer. Yellow with a plum field notebook; ideal for research, frontier ideas, and bold bets.

## Visual rules

Keep the family palette anchored in plum and warm cream, with one accent color per mascot. Preserve the ear silhouette, face proportions, small blush circles, and simple dark outline so the characters remain recognizable at small sizes.
